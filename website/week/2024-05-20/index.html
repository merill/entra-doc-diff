Week of 20 May 2024

Week in brief

Entra PIM is generally available for time-bound Dynamics 365 and Power Platform tenant admin roles

The week contained one Entra ID Message Center announcement. Microsoft announced general availability, beginning May 23, 2024, of managing admin roles for Dynamics 365 Apps and Power Platform through Microsoft Entra Privileged Identity Management (PIM). No documentation additions, updates, or removals were supplied.

  • General availability of Entra PIM role management for Dynamics 365 Apps and Power PlatformMicrosoft Entra ID — Privileged Identity Management

    This is a general availability change—not a preview or documentation clarification. Beginning May 23, 2024, Microsoft Entra PIM supports assigning tenant admin roles for these workloads for a certain time period. The announcement was published in the Microsoft 365 Message Center on May 24.

For Entra administrators

Administrators can use Microsoft Entra PIM to assign tenant admin roles for a defined period in Dynamics 365 Apps and Power Platform. The supplied announcement does not identify additional prerequisites, migration work, or configuration changes.
