Mfa Registration Campaign

12 October 2026Doc update+48 / −25 lines

In brief

The documentation explains how unrestricted, synced-only, and device-bound-only profiles use platform-specific evaluation, while AAGUID-restricted and attestation-enforced profiles suppress nudges across platforms after one eligible passkey is registered.

What Entra admins need to know

Administrators can use the profile and platform tables to understand when users will or won’t receive passkey registration nudges.

Step 2: Evaluate the platform, when required

For unrestricted, synced-only, and device-bound-only profiles, the campaign evaluates whether a qualifying credential is available for the user’s current OS and browser combination.

A credential registered on the account doesn’t necessarily suppress the nudge on every platform. It must qualify under the applicable profile and satisfy the platform-specific rule.
