Disable Agent Identities

23 October 2026Doc update+4 / −4 lines

In brief

The documented procedure now uses **Disable** > **Disable in all tenants** and specifies that the impact applies to multi-tenant blueprints.

What Entra admins need to know

Disabling such a blueprint can cause related agents in other tenants to fail or lose functionality.
