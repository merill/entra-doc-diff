Troubleshoot SMS and voice with your own telecomtelephony provider

Use this article to trace SMS and voice requests end to end with Choose Your Own Telephony Provider (CYOT), find where a request stopped, and contact the right team to resolve it: Microsoft support, your Azure team, or your telecomtelephony provider.

Stage What happens Where to look
1. Microsoft Entra sends the request Microsoft Entra requires an SMS or voice code and sends the request to your endpoint. Microsoft Entra audit logs
2. Your endpoint processes it Your Function App receives the request and sends it to your telecomtelephony provider. Function App logs
3. Your provider delivers it Your provider sends the SMS message or voice call to the user's phone. Your provider's portal
4. The user signs in The user enters the code, and Microsoft Entra completes authentication. Microsoft Entra sign-in logs
