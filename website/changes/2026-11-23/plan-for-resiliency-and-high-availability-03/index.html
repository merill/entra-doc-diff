title: Plan for resiliency and high availability description: Plan CYOT and BYOT resiliency with multi-region Azure Functions, Azure Front Door, a secondary telephony provider, and failover and recovery testing. ms.topic: concept-article ms.date: 10/30/11/23/2026 ai-usage: ai-assisted

Customer intent: As an identity administrator, I want to plan and test regional and telephony provider resiliency for my CYOT integration.

SMS and voice authentication sit on the critical path to your applications. When users rely on a phone code for multifactor authentication (MFA) or self-service password reset (SSPR), the availability of that code determines whether they can sign in.

With Choose Your Own Telephony Provider (CYOT) or Bring Your Own Telephony (BYOT), the delivery path includes components your organization owns: the Azure Function in your subscription and your telephony provider. Design these components for resiliency so authentication stays available through regional outages and provider incidents.

| Endpoint and Azure Function | Your organization | Receives the request in your Azure subscription and sends it to your provider. | | Telephony provider | Your provider | Delivers the SMS message or voice call to the user's phone. |

The CYOT guided setup creates one Azure Function in a single region, connected to one telephony provider. This setup works well for evaluation and many production workloads. For BYOT, follow your provider's published integration package for deployment details. To protect against a regional outage or a provider incident, extend it withconsider the options in this article.

!Important

Your organization manages the availability and recovery of the Azure resources and provider integration in your subscription. Test your resiliency design before you rely on it for production authentication.

[!NOTE] The multi-region and secondary-provider designs in this article are optional, sample-only guidance for CYOT and BYOT. They aren't part of the standard CYOT deployment or guaranteed features of a BYOT provider's package. Your organization is responsible for validating and maintaining these customizations.
