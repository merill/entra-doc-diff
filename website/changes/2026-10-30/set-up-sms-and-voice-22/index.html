Set up SMS and voice with your own telecom providers

Use Choose Your Own Telephony Provider (CYOT) to deliver SMS and voice authentication through your organization's telephony provider. With CYOT, you purchase a supported provider offer through Microsoft Security Store, deploy the integration in your Azure subscription, and configure Microsoft Entra to route SMS or voice authentication through your provider.

After you approve, the script creates the Microsoft Entra application, deploys and secures the Azure resources, and applies your provider's configuration. Deployment usually takes a few minutes.

You configure the authentication method policy in step 4.

Your organization is responsible for renewing and rotating the integration's application credentials and certificates.

Costs

!Important

Configure each channel with the endpoint URL and application client ID from the deployment summary. Start in evaluation mode, validate the integration, and then gradually move delivery to your telecom provider.

!Warning

CYOT updates your existing SMS or voice authentication method policy, so any change also applies to current SMS and voice users. Before you update the policy, save the full JSON response from Read the current policy.

In a production tenant, preserve theproduction, keep your existing includeTargets configuration when adding CYOT settings. Changing or removing existing targets can affect users who rely on SMS or voice authentication.
