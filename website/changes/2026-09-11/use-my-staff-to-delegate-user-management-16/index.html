Use My Staff to delegate user management

11 September 2026Retirement+8 / −14 lines

In brief

My Staff access is now determined by administrative role assignments and their administrative unit scope. The legacy settings under Manage user feature settings no longer affect behavior and are being removed.

What Entra admins need to know

Administrators should use role assignments and administrative unit scoping to control My Staff access; no action is required.
