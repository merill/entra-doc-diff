Authentication Methods Activity

Identity verification reporting

To view a user's identity verification status and history, use at least the Security Reader role.

Identity verification reporting provides account-level status and history and passkey-level provenance. These records answer different questions from the registration and usage totals in this dashboard.

  • Review identity verification status and history on the user's separate Identity verification page for account-level context.
  • Review Credential assurance and passkey credential provenance by selecting a passkey on the user's Authentication methods page and opening its details. See Credential assurance classification for Low, Medium, and High. During preview, classification and provenance are available only for passkeys.
  • Use audit logs to investigate changes to verification or provenance. Use sign-in records to review the policy evaluation and result of an access attempt separately.

Enable tenant-wide collection before viewing status and history. See Identity verification collection prerequisites. For the reporting entitlement, see Identity verification licensing; the dashboard licensing above doesn't establish eligibility for identity verification reporting.

For possible events and scenario markers, see Verified ID audit activities. Identity verification audit events are separate from the dashboard's registration and usage totals. Don't apply the dashboard's reporting latency to audit logs.

Limitations

  • The data in the report is not updated in real-time and may reflect a latency of up to 36 hours. It's possible for the reporting of a few users to fall out of that time range in rare cases. In that case, recheck the report after 24 hours.
