Lifecycle Workflows templates and categories

17 November 2026New feature+54 / −5 lines

In brief

The documentation now covers 16 built-in templates, including pre-hire identity verification and first-day employee onboarding with identity verification. It describes their triggers, configurable parameters, required profiles, and default tasks.

What Entra admins need to know

Administrators can use or customize these templates to automate employee identity verification and manager notifications. No action is required.

Lifecycle Workflows built-in templates

Lifecycle Workflows currently have 1416 built-in templates you can use or customize:

:::image type="content" source="media/lifecycle-workflow-templates/templates-list.png" alt-text="Screenshot of a list of lifecyclethe workflow template gallery showing 16 templates, including the two identity verification templates." lightbox="media/lifecycle-workflow-templates/templates-list.png":::

The list of templates is as follows:

  • Pre-hire identity verification initiation (Preview)
  • New Hire employee onboarding with identity verification (Preview)
  • Onboard pre-hire employee
  • Onboard new hire employee
  • Post-Onboarding of an employee
!Note

Lifecycle workflows enhances Microsoft Entra ID Governance's HR-driven provisioning by automating routine processes. While HR provisioning manages the creation and attribute updates of user accounts, lifecycle workflows provide additional automation of tasks.

Pre-hire identity verification initiation (Preview)

The Pre-hire identity verification initiation (Preview) template completes initial identity verification activities for new employees before their first day of work. This pre-hire phase is Day 0.

:::image type="content" source="media/lifecycle-workflow-templates/employee-identity-verification-template.png" alt-text="Screenshot of the pre-hire identity verification template summary, with a trigger seven days before employeeHireDate and two enabled tasks." lightbox="media/lifecycle-workflow-templates/employee-identity-verification-template.png":::
