Day in brief

Entra outlines late-October passkey expansion for B2B and first MFA registration

Two Microsoft Entra ID announcements point to a late-October 2026 authentication expansion. B2B users in resource tenants are slated to gain passkey registration and sign-in, while users generally will be able to register passkeys or passwordless sign-in as their first multifactor authentication method without setting up SMS or voice first. Documentation updates also remove September rollout language, and Verified ID partner material adds HYPR.

  • Entra ID · Conditional Access
    B2B users are slated to gain passkey registration and sign-in

    Starting in late October 2026, resource tenants are expected to support passkey registration and sign-in for B2B users. Internal guest users will get Microsoft Authenticator app passkeys, while external users will not. The capability is described as enabled by default and intersecting with SMS and voice retirement policies.

  • Entra ID · Conditional Access
    Passkeys can become users’ first multifactor authentication method

    A phased rollout beginning in late October 2026 will let users register passkeys or passwordless sign-in as their first multifactor authentication method, removing the need to establish SMS or voice authentication first. The announcement says no user action is required.

  • Entra ID · Authentication
    System-preferred authentication guidance removes September rollout wording

    The article date changed to October 7, 2026, and the note describing gradual Microsoft-managed deployment through September 2026 was removed. The page no longer presents that September rollout language as ongoing.

  • Entra ID · Authentication
    Registration campaign guidance drops its September completion estimate

    The article no longer says the registration campaign rollout was expected to finish by the end of September 2026 or that tenant experiences might differ during the rollout. No administrator action is indicated in the supplied update.

  • Verified ID · General
    HYPR is added to the Verified ID identity-verification partner gallery

    The gallery now lists HYPR, links to its documentation, and describes its integration for workforce identity verification. Administrators and developers comparing partners can evaluate it for remote onboarding, secure access, and account recovery.
