Entra ID will optimize passkey registration across existing registration and authentication experiences

Microsoft’s 28 July Message Center notice says Entra ID will optimize passkey registration through Registration Campaign, Authentication Strengths, and My Sign-Ins. The stated goals are to improve compliance with passkey policies and prioritize local device passkeys, with rollout planned for late August 2026.

  • Entra ID · Authentication
    Passkey registration optimization announced

    Entra ID is changing the passkey registration experience across Registration Campaign, Authentication Strengths, and My Sign-Ins to encourage compliance with passkey policies and prioritize local device passkeys.
