Day in brief

Passkey campaign guidance separates platform nudges, attestation paths, and silent SSO

The 12 October Entra ID updates focused on authentication guidance. The most consequential clarifications define how registration-campaign profiles are evaluated across platforms, where attested Authenticator passkeys can be registered, and when campaign nudges appear; examples also distinguish Windows Hello for Business from passkey profile restrictions.

  • Entra ID · Authentication
    Campaign profiles have clearer platform-specific nudge rules

    The guidance says unrestricted, synced-only, and device-bound-only profiles use platform-specific evaluation. AAGUID-restricted and attestation-enforced profiles suppress nudges across platforms after one eligible passkey is registered.

  • Entra ID · Authentication
    Attested Authenticator passkeys follow different iOS and Android paths

    iOS users can register attested passkeys only directly in the Authenticator app, while Android users can register them directly or through cross-device registration.

  • Entra ID · Authentication
    Campaign prompts are tied to interactive MFA-satisfying sign-ins

    Eligible users can see nudges during interactive sign-ins that satisfy MFA. Silent SSO events, including refresh token redemption, do not display registration-campaign nudges.

  • Entra ID · Authentication
    Windows Hello for Business is evaluated separately from passkey profiles

    The guidance states that Windows Hello for Business registration doesn’t follow passkey profile restrictions, so administrators should evaluate it separately when interpreting nudge suppression.
