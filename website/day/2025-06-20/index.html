  • Global Secure Access · Fundamentals
    Global Secure Access documents automatic cleanup of inactive connectors

    The updated Connectors article states that running connectors remain active, while unused connectors are tagged `_inactive_` and removed after 10 days of inactivity. It also says that intentional removal requires uninstalling both the Connector and Updater services and restarting the computer. This is explicit operational documentation; the record does not establish that the underlying service policy changed today.

  • ID Governance · Fundamentals

    The updated Access Reviews overview documents recurring reviews at weekly, monthly, quarterly, or annual frequencies. It also says reviewers are notified when a review starts and can approve or deny access with support from smart recommendations. This clarifies use of the existing capability rather than announcing a new feature or availability change.

    The updated Microsoft Entra ID Governance tutorial describes establishing the Darwinbox connection, configuring attribute mappings, testing account provisioning, setting account access rules, and monitoring provisioning. Fundamentals

Connectors

Updated

You don't have to manually delete connectors that are unused. When a connector is running, it remains active as it connects to the service. Unused connectors are tagged as `_inactive_` and are removed after 10 days of inactivity. If you do want to uninstall a connector, though, uninstall both the Connector service and the Updater service from the server. Restart the computer to fully remove the service.
