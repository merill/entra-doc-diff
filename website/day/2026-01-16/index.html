Day in brief

Identity Protection clarified risk remediation across authentication methods; Assignment Network documented country-code variability

16 January was primarily a documentation-maintenance day: all six supplied entries were updates, with no evidence of a new feature, preview, general-availability announcement, retirement, or Message Center notice. The most substantive update clarifies Microsoft Entra ID Protection policy behavior. A separate Entra ID update documents a platform-dependent location-data caveat. The other updates do not provide enough detail to establish a product or behavior change.

  • ID Protection · Conditional Access
    ID Protection clarifies Microsoft-managed risk remediation

    The updated Identity Protection Policies guidance says the Microsoft-managed remediation risk-based Conditional Access policy accommodates password-based and passwordless authentication. When “Require risk remediation” is selected, Entra ID Protection manages the remediation flow according to the observed threat and the user’s authentication method. This is documented policy-behavior guidance, not evidence of a new launch or availability change.

  • Entra ID · Fundamentals
    Assignment Network documents platform-dependent country codes

    The updated Entra ID guidance states that the returned country code depends on the device platform API. Its example is that one platform may report US for Puerto Rico while another reports PR. This is a location-data clarification that matters when country results are used in access policies or downstream workflows; it does not establish a new location capability.

Microsoft Purview Data Loss Prevention | Enforcement plane rename from Entra to Application

New

Microsoft Purview DLP is renaming the Enforcement plane from "Entra" to "Application" starting mid-January 2026, with no functional or user impact. Conditional Access

1

Identity Protection Policies

Updated

The Microsoft-managed remediation risk-based Conditional Access policy lets you author a risk policy that accommodates all authentication methods, including password-based and passwordless. This means that when you select "Require risk remediation" in your policy's grant controls, Microsoft Entra ID Protection manages the appropriate remediation flow based on the threat observed and the user's authentication method. For detailed steps on how to enable Microsoft-managed remediation, see [Configure risk policies](howto-identity-protection-configure-risk-policies.md#microsoft-recommendations).
